Bitcoin Cash (BCH) workshops are becoming popular on university campuses as a practical way to introduce students to cryptocurrency. These sessions teach the basics of blockchain, explain how BCH works, and offer hands-on activities like setting up wallets and making real transactions with fast, low-cost payments.
Students gain practical financial literacy, learn about digital security, and explore real-world use cases such as micro-payments, online commerce, and entrepreneurial projects. BCH workshops also inspire crypto clubs, research ideas, and innovation across different academic fields.
In a simple, interactive format, these workshops empower students to understand and engage with modern digital finance.
